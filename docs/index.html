        How the simulation works

        The rest of the season is played out 100,000 times with random results. Every percentage is the share of simulations in which that outcome happened.

        • Terms. A holdkamp is a team match; an enkeltmandskamp is one individual game within it. EKV is the number of individual games won, the first ranking criterion. MP are match points. Danmarksmester is the Danish champion.
        • Team strength blends two figures. This season's is the average rating of the players who have played for the team so far, weighted by how many individual games (K) each has played; players who have not played do not count. Last season's (2025/2026) is the same average over the players who played for the team then, weighted by their games that season but using their ratings today. For the promoted teams that is their season in 1. division, and a renamed team is followed under its old name. Last season counts fully before a team's first match and fades out as the team plays, where f is the share of its regular-season matches played:
          wlast = max(0, 1 − f / 0.66)
          strength = wlast · last season + (1 − wlast) · this season
          So with no matches played the strength is 100% last season, at 33% played it is 50/50, and from 66% played it is this season only.
        • An individual game is a 17-point match. The chance that the lower-rated player wins comes from the Danish federation's (DBgF) rating formula, where D is the rating difference:
          Pupset = 1 / (10D·√17/2000 + 1)
          With a 50-point difference the stronger player wins about 56% of the time.
        • A team match is four independent individual games. 3–1 or 4–0 gives 2 MP to the winner; 2–2 gives 1 MP each.
        • Announced lineups are used when they exist: the actual players' ratings meet board by board instead of the team averages. If one side is not yet announced, that team's strength is used.
        • Ranking follows the regulations, § 3.3: 1) EKV, 2) MP, 3) indbyrdes kampe (head-to-head: EKV, then MP in the matches between the tied teams), 4) omkamp (a play-off), modelled here as a random draw.
        • Final four: the top four after the regular season each meet the other three once, on 3 April 2027. EKV and MP are added to the regular-season totals. The leader after the final four is Danmarksmester.
        • Relegation: places 10–12 after the regular season go down directly. This is an assumption based on 2025/26, since the structure document for 2026/27 has not been published yet.
        • Next round by score. The chance of a score is the share of simulations in which the match ended that way. A team's final-four chance after, say, 3–1 is the share of the simulations ending 3–1 in which the team reached the final four. The exact score matters, not just the winner, because EKV is the first ranking criterion.
        • Not modelled: ratings stay fixed at today's values, and each team's future lineups are assumed to be as strong as its lineups so far (blended with last season's while few matches are played).
